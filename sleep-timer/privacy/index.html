Sleep Timer
privacy policy

Publisher: Amber Finch

Support: sebvannistel26@gmail.com

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Sleep Timer is a sleep-timer utility for Android TV. It operates on your TV without accounts, advertising, analytics, or an Internet permission. The app does not collect or transmit personal information or share data with others.

Settings stored on your TV

The app stores your selected duration, assigned remote-button code, dimming percentage, accessibility consent, timer deadline, boot identifier, whether the remote-button introduction was completed, a count of timers you started and whether a rating was requested, in private Android preferences. It does not store a key-event history, screen contents, or an app-usage history. These settings stay until you change them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled.

Accessibility controls

Only after your consent and activation in Android settings, the accessibility service receives remote key events to handle your selected shortcut, including while you choose one. It also receives the package name of the app in front when windows change and keeps only the latest one in memory, so that if your TV opens an app for your shortcut button, that app can be closed again with a Back action. This is never stored, logged or transmitted. It does not record or transmit key events or read screen content. The service pauses media playback, then requests sleep using Android's global lock-screen action, and displays a dimming layer and quick menu above other apps. You can disable the service in Android settings at any time. Device firmware determines which remote buttons and sleep actions work.

Tips and ratings

Tips are optional and handled entirely by Google Play: the Play Store app shows the payment screen and processes the payment under Google's own privacy policy. Sleep Timer receives only a confirmation token from Google Play to complete the tip and sees no payment details. Google's billing component may try to send its own diagnostics, but the app has no Internet permission, so nothing is sent from the app itself. After you have started a few timers, the app may once ask Google Play to show its rating sheet; whether it appears is decided by Google Play.

Other permissions

Optional precise-alarm access improves timer accuracy. Optional notification permission displays timer status, a cancellation action, and a failed sleep request. Neither is used for tracking. The billing permission lets the Play Store app take a tip you choose to give. The app requests no Internet, camera, microphone, location, shared storage, device administrator, or ADB access.

External policy and support

Opening the public policy uses a separate browser, if installed. That browser and the policy hosting service handle the connection under their own policies; Sleep Timer itself sends no app settings or key events. If you contact support, the publisher receives the information you choose to include in your message and uses it to answer your request. Do not include passwords or signing keys. The support address is on the public policy page and the Google Play listing; the app shows the publisher name and the policy address.

Changes

Any change in the app's data practices will be reflected in a dated policy update. This policy describes the Android app, not Google Play's independent services or the TV manufacturer's services.

Website hosting

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub handles website requests under its Privacy Statement. This website includes no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or embedded third-party media.
